# Fees depend on admission year and category. Re-check the Semester Fee Structure page when notices change.
Campuses: RGIPT has three campuses. Jais campus: Harbanshganj PO, Jais, Amethi, Uttar Pradesh 229304. Bengaluru campus: Kambalipura, Hosakote, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562165. Sivasagar campus: Gohain Gaon, Akhoiphutia, Sivasagar, Assam 785697.
Fee structure: RGIPT publishes a separate fee PDF for each programme and admission year (B.Tech and 5-year IDD, BBA, MSc, MBA, M.Tech, PhD) on the Semester Fee Structure page of rgipt.ac.in under Academics. Fees differ by admission year, so check the PDF for your batch.
Semester fee B.Tech: on the published B.Tech and IDD fee notice for hostel students, the fee for each semester is Rs 1,36,500 for GEN/OBC/EWS and Rs 1,11,500 for SC/ST/PwD. It includes tuition (Rs 1,00,000 for GEN/OBC/EWS, Rs 75,000 for SC/ST/PwD), examination Rs 2,000, registration Rs 1,000, amenities Rs 2,000, hostel establishment Rs 8,000, hostel seat rent Rs 8,000, medical Rs 500 and academic activities Rs 15,000. Confirm the amount for your admission year on the fee page.
First semester fee: at admission the first semester also needs one-time non-refundable charges of Rs 6,000 (admission Rs 4,000, alumni membership Rs 1,000, student welfare fund Rs 1,000) and a refundable caution money of Rs 9,000. So the first semester total is Rs 1,51,500 for GEN/OBC/EWS and Rs 1,26,500 for SC/ST/PwD on that notice.
Fee payment: bank details for admission fee payment are emailed with the offer letter. Offline payment is allowed; email the receipt with your application number to btech_admission@rgipt.ac.in. Application and registration fees are not refundable. CHECK: how current students pay semester fees and the last date.
Hostel Jais: Jais has separate hostels for boys and girls, supervised by the Council of Wardens. Rooms are shared by two students and have a bed, study table, chair, almirah, fan and tube lights. There are RO water, geysers, round-the-clock power backup and Wi-Fi and LAN, and a common room with a TV in each hostel.
Hostel rules: students must be back on campus by 8 PM unless the warden gives written permission. Visitors are not allowed in rooms and can meet students in the visitors' room from 9 AM to 8 PM. Guests cannot stay overnight. The mess is compulsory for hostellers. Heaters, coolers, irons, induction cooktops and music systems are not allowed, and students bring their own lock. Ragging, smoking, alcohol and drugs are strictly prohibited and can lead to expulsion.
Hostel leave: for outstation leave during the semester, get approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs through your department, and the hostel office then issues a gate pass. Rooms must be vacated the day after the last working day of each semester unless you have written permission.
Hostel Bengaluru: the Bengaluru campus has Block A (89 rooms) and Block B (126 rooms) for boys and a girls' hostel with 66 rooms. It has a mess, 24-hour internet, drinking water, washing machines, first aid, a gym, yoga and indoor sports rooms, TV rooms and a visitors' room. Hostel office email: HostelOffice_BengaluruCampus@rgipt.ac.in. Boys' hostel phone +91 96202 06776, girls' hostel phone +91 73551 75244.
Scholarship merit: the Institute Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for B.Tech and IDD gives a 25% tuition waiver to 10% of students in each discipline. Family income must be Rs 5 lakh or less. Renewal needs a CPI/SPI of at least 6.5, no F, I or Z grades and an income certificate each year.
Scholarship government: SC and ST students can apply for central scholarships on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). State scholarships are available through the UP, MP, Bihar and Jharkhand portals. Details and eligibility are on the Academic Scholarships page of rgipt.ac.in.
Placements: the Career Development Cell runs placements. Placement policy, placement status reports for each year, the list of recruiters and internship details are on the Placements section of rgipt.ac.in.
Library: the Central Library is listed under Resources on rgipt.ac.in. CHECK: add the library opening hours and how many books a student can borrow.
